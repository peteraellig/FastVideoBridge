PyAV, FFmpeg, x264 and x265
-------------------------
PyAV 17.1.0 source is BSD-3-Clause. The Windows PyAV wheel also bundles native
libraries with separate licenses. Its package metadata is not a license grant
for every native library in that wheel.

FFmpeg 8.1.1: the inspected DLL reports LGPL version 3 or later and is linked
with x264 and x265. The upstream PyAV vendor recipe patches FFmpeg's configure
script to move x264/x265 out of its GPL library list. This patch explains the
LGPL runtime label; it does not change the x264/x265 copyright licenses.

x264, revision b35605ace3ddf7c1a5d67a2eb553f034aef41d55, and x265 4.2 are
GPL-2.0-or-later under their open-source license grants. Their copyright
notices and full license texts are included. No commercial license or
commercial exception for these libraries is claimed by this project.
Redistribution of the combined FFmpeg/PyAV application must satisfy applicable
GPL obligations, including source availability; for the combination with
LGPL-3.0-or-later components, use the GPL-3.0-or-later-compatible route.
The original, permissively licensed FastVideo Bridge source remains available
under 0BSD. That does not remove copyleft conditions on the combined binary.
Full GPL v2/v3 and LGPL v2.1/v3 texts: licenses/native/ffmpeg/.

The native source archive includes FFmpeg, x264, x265, their vendor build
recipe and patches, and the other recipe-selected native dependencies.

OpenCV
------
OpenCV Python headless 5.0.0.93 also includes a separate FFmpeg video I/O DLL.
OpenCV/package notices are preserved under licenses/opencv/ and
licenses/python-packages/opencv_python_headless-5.0.0.93.dist-info/.
Its FFmpeg component is covered by the LGPL v2.1-or-later notices supplied
with OpenCV. The Windows DLL matches the upstream OpenCV 5.0.0 binary hash.
The matching OpenCV FFmpeg source/build tree is included in the source archive.

Other components
----------------
Python's license and bundled notices are in licenses/python/.
Python package license texts and bundled notices, including those supplied
with NumPy, cryptography, PyOpenSSL, aiortc and pylibsrtp, are preserved under
licenses/python-packages/. See licenses/python-packages.json for the inventory.
Additional native codec/library notices are under licenses/native/.
GCC runtime files have GPL terms with the GCC Runtime Library Exception;
that exception is included. libiconv has LGPL terms. zlib and winpthreads
carry their own permissive notices, which are also included.

Go dependency licenses remain in gateway/vendor and gateway/third_party_licenses.

obtain those separately under their vendors' terms.
